Tactune

A smarter way to practice music.

Tactune is a web-based sheet music player designed to make practicing more efficient and distraction-free. Upload your sheet music, choose your desired tempo, and let Tactune automatically navigate through your score.

By combining intelligent score recognition, automatic scrolling and integrated practice tools, Tactune eliminates the need for manual page navigation while playing.

Website: https://tactune.github.io

Features

Feature	Description
PDF Import	Upload your own sheet music in PDF format.
Score Recognition	Automatically detects musical staves and measures.
Intelligent Scrolling	Automatically navigates through your sheet music based on its musical structure.
Tempo Control	Set your preferred playback speed using BPM.
Tap Tempo	Determine the desired tempo by tapping.
Metronome	Practice with an integrated metronome and configurable count-in.
Measure Selection	Start practicing from a specific measure.
Loop Mode	Repeat selected sections of your sheet music.
Tempo Trainer	Gradually increase the tempo during repeated practice.
Music Library	Save, search and organize your songs.
Customization	Adjust zoom, appearance and display settings.
Stage Mode	Use a distraction-free interface for playing.

How It Works

1. Upload your sheet music as a PDF.
2. Recognize musical staves and measures automatically.
3. Select a starting measure or position.
4. Configure your desired tempo and metronome settings.
5. Play while Tactune automatically navigates through your score.

Technology

Tactune is designed as a browser-based application. It can be accessed without installing additional desktop software.

Privacy

Uploaded sheet music is processed locally in the browser. Optional account features provide additional functionality for managing saved songs.

Development

Tactune is continuously being developed and improved. New features, usability improvements and refinements are part of the ongoing development process.
