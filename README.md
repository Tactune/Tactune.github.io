Tactune

A smarter way to follow sheet music.

Tactune is a web-based sheet music player designed to make practicing music easier and more convenient. Upload your sheet music, select your desired tempo, and let Tactune automatically scroll through the score while you play.

No more manual scrolling. Just focus on the music.

Website: https://tactune.github.io

⸻

Overview

When practicing with digital sheet music, manually scrolling through pages can interrupt your concentration and make playing less comfortable.

Tactune solves this problem by automatically scrolling through your uploaded sheet music at a tempo of your choice. This allows musicians to keep their hands on their instrument and their attention on the performance.

Features

* Sheet Music Upload – Upload your own digital sheet music.
* Adjustable Tempo – Choose the tempo that suits your practice session.
* Automatic Scrolling – Sheet music scrolls automatically at the selected tempo.
* Uninterrupted Practice – No need to manually scroll while playing.
* Browser-Based – Access Tactune directly through your web browser.

How It Works

1. Upload – Select and upload your sheet music.
2. Set the Tempo – Choose your preferred playback speed.
3. Start – Begin your practice session.
4. Play – Follow the automatically scrolling sheet music without manual interaction.
